Immersive Inventory

Items on your cursor have weight.

Pick up an item and it hangs from the exact spot you grabbed it by, swinging like a pendulum. A torch held by the handle flips upside down, a lantern hangs from its ring, a sharp flick of the mouse sends a sword spinning.

Works out of the box with vanilla and modded items alike. Liquids, containers and plain blocks stay still.

Pairs perfectly with Inventory Particles: particles follow the swinging item, a flipped torch still burns upward, and weapons leave a trail when swung.

Client-side only. No server install needed.
